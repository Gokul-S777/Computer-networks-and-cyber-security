# Computer-networks-and-cyber-security
Q20 – Forensic Investigation of a Simulated Cyber Incident

1. Introduction

Cyber incidents can happen when a user opens a suspicious link, downloads an unknown file, or provides account information to an unsafe website. Digital forensics helps in understanding what happened during such an incident by collecting and analyzing digital evidence.

This project presents a simulated cyber incident and its forensic investigation. The incident is based on a phishing email received by an employee. The investigation focuses on identifying suspicious activity, examining available evidence, understanding the possible impact, and suggesting suitable security measures.

This is a simulated academic investigation and does not involve any real organization's systems or data.

2. Incident Scenario

In this simulated incident, an employee receives an email that appears to be a normal account verification message. The email contains a link asking the employee to verify their account.

The employee clicks the suspicious link without checking its authenticity. After this activity, unusual login activity is noticed. The incident is therefore considered a possible phishing-related security incident.

The investigation is carried out by examining the available email information, system logs, network activity, and other relevant evidence.

Objectives

- To analyze the simulated cyber incident.
- To identify suspicious activities.
- To collect and examine digital evidence.
- To understand the possible cause of the incident.
- To identify the possible impact.
- To recommend security measures to prevent similar incidents.

3. Investigation Methodology

The investigation follows a simple forensic process.

3.1 Incident Identification

The suspicious email and unusual login activity were identified as the main indicators of the incident.

3.2 Evidence Collection

Relevant evidence such as email details, security logs, network activity, and suspicious file information was considered for investigation.

3.3 Evidence Preservation

The collected evidence was stored separately to reduce the possibility of accidental modification. Hash values can also be generated for important evidence files to check their integrity.

3.4 Evidence Analysis

The collected information was examined for unusual login times, suspicious network connections, and other activities that could be related to the incident.

3.5 Reporting

The observations and findings from the investigation were documented and used to prepare the final forensic investigation report.

Evidence is an important part of a forensic investigation. Different sources can provide information about what happened during an incident.

The following evidence can be collected for this simulated investigation:

1. Suspicious email information.

4. Suspicious file or process information.
5. Hash values of important evidence files.

6. Evidence Analysis

After collecting the evidence, each item is examined to understand whether it is related to the simulated incident.

Evidence| Observation| Finding
Suspicious email| Contains an unexpected link| Possible phishing attempt
Security logs| Unusual login activity| Possible unauthorized access
Network activity| Unexpected communication| Requires further investigation
File information| Suspicious activity may be present| Possible security threat

The suspicious email is considered the possible starting point of the incident. The unusual login activity after the user interacted with the email is also treated as an important indicator.

Since this is a simulated investigation, the findings are limited to the evidence available in the scenario.

8. Findings

The main findings from the simulated investigation are:

- The email showed characteristics of a possible phishing message.
- The user interacted with the suspicious link.
- Unusual login activity was observed after the interaction.
- The available evidence indicates a possible attempt to gain unauthorized access.
- Additional evidence would be required to confirm the complete attack path.

9. Possible Impact

The simulated incident could potentially result in:

- Unauthorized access to an account.
- Exposure of account information.
- Loss of confidentiality.
- Possible installation of malicious software.
- Further misuse of compromised credentials.

The actual impact cannot be confirmed without additional evidence.

10. Recommendations

The following measures can help prevent similar incidents:

1. Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for important accounts.
2. Provide regular phishing awareness training to users.
3. Avoid clicking unknown or suspicious links.
4. Monitor security logs for unusual login activity.
5. Keep antivirus and endpoint security software updated.
6. Use strong and unique passwords.
7. Report suspicious emails to the appropriate security team.
8. Maintain a proper incident response procedure.
9. Preserve digital evidence carefully during an incident.
10. Regularly review account access and authentication activity.

11. Conclusion

The investigation showed that email information, security logs, and network activity can provide useful information during a cyber incident. It also showed the importance of preserving evidence and maintaining proper records during a forensic investigation.

Overall, this project helped in understanding the basic process of digital forensic investigation and the importance of identifying suspicious activity at an early stage.
